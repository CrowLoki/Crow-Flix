REUSE licence-file location. The “only” suffix is intentional: the project does
not grant an automatic option to use a later version of the GNU Affero General
Public License.

people to use over a network, review and satisfy the corresponding-source and

## Crow brand assets

The following are not licensed under the AGPL:

- `public/assets/brand/**`

Those files include the Crow mascot, Crow head, Crow Bitfeather fonts, Crow
Talon cursors, and icons derived from the Crow head. They are licensed only
under `LicenseRef-Crow-Brand`, whose complete terms are in
[`LICENSES/LicenseRef-Crow-Brand.txt`](LICENSES/LicenseRef-Crow-Brand.txt).

The custom licence permits the assets to accompany authentic, unmodified
CrowFlix source and official CrowFlix releases. It does not grant permission to
use the Crow identity to brand a fork, another product, a service, merchandise,
or an endorsement. A fork may keep using the AGPL-covered software after
removing or replacing the Crow brand assets.

See [`BRAND-ASSETS.md`](BRAND-ASSETS.md) for the asset inventory, provenance,
and practical guidance.

## Third-party software

CrowFlix depends on third-party packages. Each dependency remains under its own
licence. The generated files `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and
`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` identify the dependency versions and reproduce the
licence material distributed with release builds.

The AGPL licence for CrowFlix does not replace, narrow, or expand any
third-party licence.

The repository does not grant rights to any channel name, programme,
broadcast, stream, logo, listing, website, or other third-party content. Users
